Inspection-First Home Protection Services

Core services only: inspect, exclude, correct, and prevent repeat access.

Home Protection & Access-Point Inspection

A focused inspection of visible and accessible areas with attention on entry points, roofline/vent conditions, moisture indicators, and exterior gaps.

  • Condition notes
  • Photo documentation
  • Prioritized recommendations

Wildlife Access & Exclusion

Wildlife activity is usually a symptom of access. We focus on identifying and correcting entry points, not just reacting to the animal.

Crawlspace & Moisture Awareness

We observe visible crawlspace and moisture indicators, then recommend practical next steps or specialist referral when needed.

Minor Home-Protection Repairs

Minor repairs may include vent screening, gap sealing, trim repair, and small soffit/fascia correction where safe and within scope.

Seasonal Prevention Plan

Seasonal check-ins help catch visible changes early and reduce preventable damage over time.
