Inspection-First Home Protection Services

Most home problems start small: a gap under trim, a loose vent, moisture in the crawlspace, a rub mark near an entry point, or a pattern no one has documented. Our services begin with observation and lead to practical next steps.

Home Protection & Access-Point Inspection

A practical inspection of visible and accessible areas of the home and property envelope, focused on wildlife entry points, moisture concerns, crawlspace and attic indicators, exterior gaps, vents, doors, windows, roofline conditions, and maintenance vulnerabilities.

Starting at $300-$500 depending on home size, access, and complexity.

  • Observed conditions
  • Photo documentation
  • Prioritized recommendations
  • Prevention opportunities
  • Possible THD follow-up work
  • Referrals where licensed specialists are needed

Wildlife Access & Exclusion

Wildlife activity is often a symptom of access. We focus on identifying and correcting entry points, not just reacting to the animal. We do not guarantee elimination of all future wildlife activity because animals can create new openings or use concealed routes.

Crawlspace & Moisture Awareness

We observe visible crawlspace and moisture indicators and recommend practical next steps. Further evaluation may be needed for mold, structural, HVAC, plumbing, or drainage concerns.

Minor Home-Protection Repairs

Small repairs may include vent screening, gap sealing, trim repair, minor soffit or fascia repair, and basic access correction where safe and within scope.

Seasonal Prevention Plan

Quarterly or seasonal check-ins help keep the home envelope documented and maintained. The goal is to catch visible changes early and reduce preventable damage over time.

Property Vulnerability Walkthrough

A practical, civilian-scope walkthrough of visible exterior vulnerabilities such as lighting, visibility, access points, doors, windows, vents, and maintenance readiness. This is not a formal security assessment.
