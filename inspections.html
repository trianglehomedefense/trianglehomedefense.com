The Inspection Turns Problems Into Decisions

An inspection gives the homeowner a clear picture of visible conditions at the time of the visit. It does not promise to find every hidden issue, but it does create a practical record of what can be seen, what may matter, and what should happen next.

Process

1. Contact

Tell us what you are seeing, hearing, or concerned about.

2. Site Visit

We walk the property and inspect visible and accessible areas.

3. Photo Documentation

We photograph relevant conditions and organize them by area.

4. Findings

We identify access points, moisture indicators, maintenance concerns, and possible next steps.

5. Recommendations

Recommendations may include THD repair or exclusion work, prevention planning, monitoring, or referral to licensed specialists.

6. Follow-Up

You receive a practical action list and can decide what to address first.

Inspection areas

  • Roofline
  • Soffit and fascia
  • Attic access where safe
  • Vents
  • Crawlspace entry and visible crawlspace areas
  • Foundation perimeter
  • Doors and windows
  • Utility penetrations
  • Drainage indicators
  • Exterior gaps
  • Visible wildlife signs
  • Maintenance history notes

Limitations

  • Visible and accessible areas only
  • No destructive testing
  • No promise of discovering hidden defects
  • No promise of future wildlife elimination
  • Referrals for licensed trades when needed
