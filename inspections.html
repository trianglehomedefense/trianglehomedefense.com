The Inspection Turns Problems Into Decisions

An inspection gives the homeowner a clear picture of visible conditions at the time of the visit. It does not promise to find every hidden issue, but it does create a practical record of what can be seen, what may matter, and what should happen next.

Process

  1. Contact: tell us what you are seeing, hearing, or concerned about.
  2. Site Visit: we walk the property and inspect visible and accessible areas.
  3. Photo Documentation: we photograph relevant conditions and organize them by area.
  4. Findings: we identify access points, moisture indicators, maintenance concerns, and possible next steps.
  5. Recommendations: may include THD repair/exclusion work, prevention planning, monitoring, or referral to licensed specialists.
  6. Follow-Up: you receive a practical action list and can decide what to address first.

Inspection Areas

  • Roofline
  • Soffit/fascia
  • Attic access where safe
  • Vents
  • Crawlspace entry and visible crawlspace areas
  • Foundation perimeter
  • Doors/windows
  • Utility penetrations
  • Drainage indicators
  • Exterior gaps
  • Visible wildlife signs
  • Maintenance history notes

Limitations

Inspections are limited to visible and accessible areas. We do not perform destructive testing, do not guarantee discovery of hidden defects, and do not guarantee elimination of all future wildlife activity. Further evaluation may be needed, and work outside our scope is referred to licensed professionals.
