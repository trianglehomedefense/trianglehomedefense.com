Triangle Home Defense helps homeowners understand what is happening around the home before small issues become expensive problems. We inspect visible and accessible areas, document access points and moisture concerns, and help you decide what to do next.

Start with an inspection

The cleanest first step is a Home Protection & Access-Point Inspection.

We look at visible and accessible parts of the home envelope: roofline, vents, crawlspace, attic access, foundation, doors, windows, exterior gaps, and common entry points. You receive practical notes, photo documentation, and a prioritized action list.

Recommendations are based on visible conditions observed at the time of inspection. Services may be limited by access, safety, weather, and site conditions.

What we do

Practical protection for the home as a system.

Inspection & Documentation

We inspect, photograph, and document visible conditions so homeowners can make clear decisions.

Wildlife Access & Exclusion

Wildlife is often the first sign that a home has open access points. We identify those openings and perform minor exclusion work where appropriate.

Crawlspace & Moisture Awareness

Moisture, airflow, and crawlspace conditions affect the whole home. We document visible concerns and recommend next steps.

Prevention & Seasonal Checks

Recurring prevention helps catch small issues before they become expensive repairs.

Minor Home-Protection Repairs

When within scope, we repair small gaps, damaged trim, vents, and access points that expose the home.

Stewardship-Minded Property Care

A home is part of a larger system: structure, soil, water, wildlife, family use, and maintenance history.

Why this is different

Not generic pest control, ordinary contracting, or fear-based home selling.

Triangle Home Defense is inspection-first and documentation-heavy. We help homeowners understand the property as a system, reduce preventable damage, and maintain continuity over time.

Practical defense, not fear

For us, defense means awareness, prevention, access-point correction, documentation, and continuity. It does not mean personal protection services, public-safety authority, or promises that every future problem can be eliminated.
